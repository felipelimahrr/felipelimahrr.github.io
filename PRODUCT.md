## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (user's choice). No framework, no build step; must host on any static host.

## Users

Business owners, experts and brands that need video for social media. Most open the site on a phone, arriving from WhatsApp or Instagram. They include professionals building a personal brand, mentors, clinics and local companies. They arrive from Instagram (@ofelipesm), WhatsApp links or referrals, evaluate whether Felipe's work is good enough to hire, and want to see real results before talking.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio site for Felipe Lima (FL), positioned first as a **video maker & creator**: recording, lighting, scripting and editing of video and photo for brands and experts. Social media management, branding design and strategic planning appear as complementary services, not the headline.

Success: a visitor sees the work, trusts the quality, and moves to a conversation on WhatsApp or Instagram.

## Positioning

A creator who both films the material and knows how to make it grow organically: 5+ years working with organic growth methods, with case results across real clients (from a single expert to a full mentorship team; a page grown from zero to 25k+ followers organically). Video craft is the lead; the strategy background is what makes the videos perform.
